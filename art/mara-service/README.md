# Mara’s Service — Concept Images

These images are visual concept art for a new, silent service/memorial scene for Mara in *Nobody’s Witness*. They are not integrated into the film or game.

The sequence imagines a small, intimate memorial in Japan, held after the immediate events of Mara’s death. It includes Vera, her American mother, Jack, Kaneko, Okada, and members of the Hive. The mourners wear respectful formal clothes; Vera is the only person with a blue umbrella. The images follow the existing film’s rainy, restrained cinematic look and use Mara’s rescued drawing as a personal memorial object.
