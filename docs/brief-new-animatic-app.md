---

Build a new web app, **an animatic and keyframe editor for films**, in a new repository.

## What it is for

A director makes a film from still pictures, recorded voices, sound effects and music.

**The app shows the film as one timeline of keyframes,** where a keyframe is one picture on one beat of the script. For
each keyframe, the director can:
- pick the picture from every image in the project, or upload a new one;
- set its camera move and its effects;
- hear it with its voice and sound.

**It plays the whole film as an animatic and exports it as an MP4.**

**There are no branching choices.** A film plays straight through.

## Import from Scarlet Reel, with the same effects

**The source.** The films come from Scarlet Reel (`sigooding/scarlett-witness`), a React/Vite visual-novel engine with a
Studio. Its film Nobody's Witness has about 250 scene nodes, about 1,700 beats and about 800 pictures.

**Import two things:**
1. **A Studio export:** a `.reel.json` file, the `PortableStoryBundle` in `src/studio/exportBundle.ts`. It holds
   `{ story, assets, missing }`; the media is inline or referenced.
2. **The compiled film itself:** the `Story` object in `src/data/nobodysWitnessFilm.ts`. It is generated by
   `tools/build-film-story.mjs` and can be read as JSON.

The types are in `src/types.ts` (`Story`, `SceneNode`, `DialogueBeat`, `CinematicSettings`). Read them fully before
writing the importer.

**How the importer maps a film:**
- **Running order.** Start at the story's start scene. Walk each scene node's `dialogueBeats` in order, then follow the
  link onward:
  - the last beat's `afterBeatTargetSceneId`;
  - else the node's `autoAdvanceTargetSceneId`;
  - else its first choice's target.

  `src/game/inheritedMusic.ts` walks the same way. Song and cutscene nodes (timed shots with captions) are part of the
  order too.
- **One keyframe per beat.** Its picture is the beat's `background` or, if the beat has none, the picture carried from the
  beat before (mark it "carried").
- **The beat's fields come across:** text, speaker (`characterId`), voice take (`audioUrl`), its own camera (`cinematic`),
  transition and its speed, hold and revert, sound effect (`sfxUrl`, `sfxVolume`), and any timing fields.
- **A scene node's fields go to a "scene" group around its keyframes:**
  - its heading (`prose`);
  - its picture and camera (`background`, `cinematic`);
  - music: `musicUrl`, `musicMode`, `musicLoop`, `musicLevel`, `musicFadeSeconds`, `musicFadeOutSeconds`;
  - ambience: `ambientAudio`, recorded layers written `preset+file:<url>`; parse them as
    `src/studio/ambientParts.ts` does;
  - `particles`, `letterbox` and reading pace.
- **Paths.** Media paths are kept, and resolved against a folder the user points at: the repository's `public/` folder.
  `asset:` ids are resolved from the bundle's `assets`.
- **Missing media.** Anything missing is listed on screen after the import, never dropped silently.

**The same effects means the same code.** Do not re-implement the effects from their descriptions. Copy Scarlet Reel's
rendering code into a `vendor/scarlet-reel/` folder (it is the same author's code), keep it as unchanged as you can, and
render every picture through it:
- `src/cinematic.ts`: camera modes, focal point, zoom, grades, lights, lens flare;
- `src/game/DepthParallax.tsx` and the depth-map fields (`depthMap`, `depthMotion`, `depthAmount`, `depthSeconds`, cut-out
  layers);
- `src/game/AmbientParticles.tsx`: rain, drizzle, motes;
- `src/game/audioFade.ts`, plus the music and ducking logic in `src/game/Game.tsx` (`startTrack`, `fadeAudio`, the duck
  factor). Copy the parts you need, not the whole file;
- the CSS for these from `src/index.css`;
- `src/game/animatic.tsx`: reading-pace timing (`readingSeconds`), the scene and beat marks.

**Prove it:** render the same five keyframes in Scarlet Reel and in the new app, and compare the frames (pixel diff under
a small threshold). Choose five that cover:
- a plain picture;
- a depth-map camera;
- a grade;
- rain;
- a crossfade.

## The editor

- **The timeline.** One row per keyframe in running order, with a thumbnail, the scene mark (e.g. 77A), the beat number,
  the speaker and the first line.
  - **Scenes** are labels that fold.
  - **Windowing:** the list is virtualised (1,700 rows must scroll smoothly).
  - **Filters:** by scene, by text, and "no picture of its own".
- **A keyframe's panel:**
  - **Preview.** A large live preview of the keyframe playing.
  - **Picture.**
    - The image browser lists every image in the project, grouped by folder, with search, and with hover preview.
    - Upload by button or by drag and drop.
  - **Camera.**
    - The move, the focal point (click on the preview to set it) and the zoom.
    - The start and end framing, with easing. The camera can have its own keyframes inside one shot: start, middle, end.
  - **Effects, all of them:** depth map and its move, grade, lights, lens flare, particles, transition and its speed.
  - **Sound.** The voice take (play and trim the offset), the sound effect and its volume.
  - **Hold time.** If the beat has no voice, it holds for the reading pace.
- **Edits across keyframes:** add, duplicate, delete and reorder keyframes inside a scene. Split a beat into two pictures,
  each with its share of the text.
- **Tracks under the timeline:**
  - music, with fade in, fade out, overlap with the next track, and its level;
  - ambience layers;
  - effects, as markers.

  Drag a cue to move it.
- **Undo and redo** for everything. Autosave the project (IndexedDB), and keep named snapshots.
- **Keys:** ↑/↓ for the previous and next keyframe, Space to play from here, J/K/L to shuttle, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Z.

## The animatic

- **Play.** Play from any keyframe in real time, with all effects and sound, and the voices ducking the music. There is
  never a choice window, and no scene "opening" step: each keyframe starts on its own picture and camera.
- **Timing.** Each keyframe lasts its voice take plus a small tail, or its reading time, or a set hold. A music cue can
  set its scenes' cuts to land on the beat (`musicSync` in Scarlet Reel's `src/film/studio-edits.json`).
- **Export:**
  - MP4, 1080p, 24 fps, H.264, AAC;
  - burnt-in captions as an option, plus an SRT;
  - the scene and beat mark in a corner as an option;
  - a range (from keyframe to keyframe) or the whole film;
  - with progress and cancel.

  Render with the same code as the preview: drive the page in headless Chromium frame by frame, with ffmpeg encoding, as
  Scarlet Reel's `tools/export-animatic.mjs` does. Read that file first: it solves fixed-step time, audio mixing and
  slow frames. Don't render with a second, simplified renderer.

## Back to Scarlet Reel

- **Export a `.reel.json`** (`PortableStoryBundle`) that Scarlet Reel's Studio imports: the same story, with each beat's
  `background`, `cinematic`, `sfxUrl` and the scene fields updated from the edits.
- **For Nobody's Witness, also export a patch** in the shape of `src/film/studio-edits.json`'s existing keys (Scarlet
  Reel's builder applies these):
  - `pictures: [{ scene, find, file }]`, a picture on the beat whose text contains `find`;
  - `pictureLook["<file name without extension>"]`, a picture's camera and effects;
  - `sfx: [{ scene, find, file, volume }]`.

  Make each `find` unique within its scene. Say where a change has no matching key, rather than dropping it.

## Rules

- **Pictures are never made smaller,** recompressed or renamed. An upload is stored byte for byte. If it is not 16:9, crop
  it to 16:9; if it is under 1920×1080, enlarge it. Never shrink.
- **Never overwrite a file.** A new picture gets a new name. Replacing one keeps the old one (`archive/…--v<N>`).
- **No depth map alone on a picture with people in it.** It stretches them; Scarlet Reel uses cut-outs for that
  (`docs/depth-camera.md` there).
- **Everything the app shows is the real result,** not an approximation, or it is labelled "preview only".

## Stack

**Vite, React and TypeScript, as Scarlet Reel uses,** so its code drops in.
- **Storage.** IndexedDB for projects, and the File System Access API (or a small local Node server under `npm run dev`)
  for reading the media folder and writing exports.
- **Export.** ffmpeg on the machine, found with `FFMPEG=` or on the PATH. Chromium through Playwright.

## Done means

1. **Import.** Importing Nobody's Witness gives the same keyframe count as the film's beats in running order, with scene
   77A between 77 and 78, and lists any missing media.
2. **Parity.** The five-keyframe comparison with Scarlet Reel passes.
3. **Editing.** Change a picture, a camera move and a sound effect, undo them, and redo them.
4. **Export.** A one-minute MP4 from scene 77 has its voices, music fades and rain, and runs within half a second of its
   preview.
5. **Round trip.** Export a `.reel.json` and import it into Scarlet Reel's Studio: the changed beat shows the new picture.
6. **Tests.** Unit tests for the importer, the running order, the timing and the patch writer, plus one browser test that
   plays a keyframe and checks a frame.
7. **README.** How to import, edit, play and export, and what is not yet the same as Scarlet Reel.
