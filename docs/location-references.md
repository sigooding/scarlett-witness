## 2. The service road behind the Hive (scenes 67 and 70): who stands where

**Sources.** This comes from the screenplay and from what most of the pictures already show (234, 389, 390, 237). For the
look of the road, `sheets/hive-exterior-night.jpg` has a panel of it ("Rear service road at NIGHT").

**The place.**
- A narrow road under the elevated railway.
- **One side:** the Hive's back wall with its narrow steel back door and a lamp over it. Further up the same side, closed
  garages; the 8:52 clock sign is above one of them.
- **The other side:** the row of massive concrete pillars, with darkness behind them.
- **One sodium lamp,** by the pillar where the car waits.

**The car.**
- The grey sedan waits beside the pillars, twenty metres from the back door, with its engine running.
- Seen from the door end, its nose points left, toward the Hive's side of the road.
- Its near rear door is the one the driver opens for them, as in 390 and 237.

**The driver.**
- He stands at that open rear door, facing them.
- He bows and says "Shitsurei shimasu.", then murmurs "Position." into his sleeve.
- When the shooting starts, he pulls a gun from his coat there.

**The shooter.**
- In the dark between the pillars, across the road from Jack and Mara, outside the lamp's light. The screenplay says no
  more than "From the dark between the pillars".
- 394 shows two muzzle flashes in two places. One shooter or two is the director's call.

**Jack and Mara:**
1. They come out of the back door and walk toward the car (389).
2. About halfway, ten metres out, come the driver's words, and Mara stops dead (391, 392, 237).
3. "Run!": Jack shoves her back toward the door (393). Rounds come from the pillars, and they get down behind the
   dumpster.
   - **Where the dumpster is:** against the Hive wall, between the door and where they stopped, about five metres from
     the door.
   - **Which side they crouch on:** the door side, so the dumpster is between them and the pillars (395).
4. Mara breaks for the door: five metres, three. The shot. She falls through the doorway (396, 397).
5. Jack goes after her, low, as bullets hit the doorframe above his head (398).

**From the door end, looking up the road** (where most of the scene is seen from):
- the Hive wall, the dumpster and the garages are on the LEFT;
- the pillars and the lamp are on the RIGHT;
- the car is ahead on the right, its nose pointing left.

**Pictures to fix:**

| Picture | Plays on | What is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| 396 | "Mara breaks for the Hive door. Five metres. Three." | She runs away from the lit door, down the road toward the car and the driver. She should run to the door, with the dumpster behind her. |
| 394 | "From the dark between the pillars: muzzle flashes." | The car is seen from behind, pointing into the pillars, at the right of the frame. Everywhere else it is side-on, its nose toward the Hive's side. It also shows two muzzle flashes (see "The shooter"). |
| 395 | "Jack draws Mara down behind a dumpster." | The car is in plain view beside them. The dumpster should be between them and the pillars. |
| 393 | "Jack shoves Mara back toward the Hive door." | A small fault: Jack stands by the door rather than halfway up the road. |

Fine as they are: 234, 389, 390, 391, 392, 237, 397 and 398.
