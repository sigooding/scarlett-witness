## Revised master prompt / continuity brief

Create a fresh 16:9 still set for scenes 72–76 of *Nobody's Witness*, using the current scene source (`src/film/story.json`) as the backbone: the hotel-lounge call (72), Vera running through rainy Tokyo toward the Hive (73), her street encounter with Jack (74), the empty still-life aftermath (75), and Vera alone in her apartment (76). Keep the story emotionally readable and physical geography credible; simplify any beat that would make a frame confusing. The inn-escape note is not applicable.

**Use the original Vera as the character identity reference**, especially `public/film/img/s72/70-the-call.jpg` for her face and `public/film/img/s73/69-vera-runs.jpg` for her wet exterior look. Match the same face and hair rather than inventing a similar blonde: same face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, fair complexion, age, and shoulder-length blonde hair/fringe. Keep her deep-burgundy sleeveless evening dress and phone; she begins dry indoors, becomes rain-soaked outside, loses one heel during the run, and remains with one shoe/one bare foot until the apartment scene. Jack is the same tired, middle-aged man with short salt-and-pepper hair, light stubble, pale shirt, and charcoal raincoat; he is wet and marked from the Hive, and keeps a respectful distance after the confrontation. In the lounge, show the bartender when his line occurs and include the pianist actually playing; later street and apartment scenes should remain empty of extra people.

Keep the Tokyo street as one coherent space: a straight rain-slick route under the elevated railway with vending machines on one side and the closed shop/awning opposite; preserve travel direction, scale, eyelines, and plausible distances between the crossing, the Hive direction, Vera, and Jack. Use grounded contemporary neo-noir photography, slate/blue-green night shadows, practical amber interior light or cold vending light, natural exposure, subtle film grain, realistic anatomy and objects, and restrained rain. Use existing frames sparingly as identity/style references only; no source image is to be reused or overwritten. Avoid accidental extra limbs/props, impossible reflections, unreadable signage, logos, captions, borders, and watermarks.
