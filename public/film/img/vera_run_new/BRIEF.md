# Vera's night run, lobby to home (scenes 72–76): brief for a new picture set

Written 17 October 2026 for another image agent. Nothing in the film uses this folder yet.

WHERE
Repository: sigooding/scarlett-witness, branch main.
Every path below is relative to public/film/img/ in that repository.
Make a new folder, vera_run_new/, and save every picture in it under the exact filename given.
Style sheets go in vera_run_new/style-sheets/.

THE SEQUENCE
Nobody's Witness is a contemporary neo-noir film set in Tokyo. Just before midnight, Vera Voss (29), in an old wine-red silk dress of her mother's, has waited two hours in an old hotel lounge for Jack, who never came. Detective Ishida phones: her sister Mara was found dead tonight, and Jack had been hiding her. Vera walks out through the hotel lobby into the rain and runs, badly, in heels, toward the elevated railway and the Hive where Mara died. At a crossing she loses a shoe. On an empty street under the tracks she meets Jack, his hands still dark with Mara's blood. She hits him, breaks, and he cannot say anything. Then a few still frames of the empty night, and Vera at home on the floor of her apartment, calling her mother and saying nothing.
All of it plays to one song, a picture every 4 to 5 seconds. Every picture must look like the same night, the same woman, the same dress and the same streets as the pictures next to it.

RULES
1. Follow the character references exactly. Attach each person's reference whenever they appear and match their face, age, hair and costume. Never invent a new face. If a face drifts from its reference, regenerate.
2. Keep the places consistent: the same hotel, the same avenue, the same crossing, the same street under the tracks and the same apartment every time, laid out as described below. Attach the location sheet for every shot. Never mirror a location.
3. Keep screen direction. On the run Vera always moves from screen left to screen right, with the railway ahead of her (screen right). From in front she runs toward camera; from behind, away from it. On the street under the tracks Vera is always screen left and Jack screen right.
4. Keep her state right through the night (see HER STATE THROUGH THE NIGHT).
5. When Vera is in frame, her wine-red dress is the strongest colour in the picture. The pale blue umbrella is the only blue object in the whole sequence.
6. End every prompt with the STYLE LINE.
7. Use only the references listed here. The pictures now in s72/ to s76/ are being replaced: use them for the places only, never for Vera's face or the dress.
8. No legible text except the phone screen (ISHIDA, MOM) and the numbers on the lounge clock. No signage, logos, watermarks, borders or letterbox bars.
9. Format: JPEG, 16:9, at least 1920x1080. Bigger is better. Never smaller, never downscaled.
10. Never overwrite or delete an existing file. If a filename already exists, stop and report it.
11. Work only in the scarlett-witness repository. Do not touch the PlayFrame or nobodys-witness repositories, and do not wire the pictures into the film (src/film/story.json, studio-edits): that is done separately.

WHO
- Vera Voss, 29, American: shoulder-length blonde hair with a curtain fringe, blue-grey eyes, fair skin. A wine-red silk midi dress (soft cowl neckline, wide shoulder straps, bias-cut skirt to mid-calf), wine-red low-heeled pumps, and a small black velvet handbag on a thin strap over her left shoulder. No coat, no umbrella. Her black phone is in her right hand.
- Jack: as on his sheet; tonight soaked, long black overcoat, off-white open-collar shirt, his hands dark with dried blood.
- In the lounge only: the pianist (60s, white jacket), a bartender (white shirt, black waistcoat) and a few late guests. In the lobby: an old night clerk. No one else anywhere in the sequence.

HER STATE THROUGH THE NIGHT
- In the lounge and the lobby (72-01 to 73-01): dry, hair done, lipstick, both shoes.
- From 73-02 (outside): wetter in every shot. By 73-06 she is soaked, her hair plastered, her mascara running.
- Shoes: both pumps until 73-14. From 73-15 on, the RIGHT shoe is gone (left in the puddle at the crossing) and her right foot is bare.
- From 74-13 on: the knees and hem of the dress are dirty from the wet road.
- At home (76): still the ruined red dress, mascara dried in streaks, the left pump still on, the right foot bare and dirty.
- The phone: in her right hand from the call to the crossing. The screen is still lit with ISHIDA in 72-02 and 73-04, dark after that.
- The lighter: she drops it into her bag in 72-03. It comes out again only at home (76-07).
- The bag: on the bar, then on her left shoulder for the whole run, then on the floor at home.
- The pale blue umbrella stays leaning against the bar stool in the lounge (72-05, 75-02).

LIGHT
The lounge: amber lamps. The lobby: warm brass lamps. Outside: streetlights and the cold white of vending machines, heavy steady rain in every exterior. Home: dark, rain on the window, the phone the only light on her face.
